# 1. Overused Vocabulary

Language models favour certain words far more than human writers do. Kobak et al. measured this in PubMed abstracts: after 2023 the excess vocabulary was almost entirely *style* words (66% verbs, 14% adjectives), whereas earlier shifts, such as during Covid, were *content* nouns. A single word proves nothing; several in one paragraph is a strong sign.

## 1.1 Words with the largest jump in scientific abstracts (Kobak et al.)

Rare words with very high frequency ratios in 2024:
- **delve / delves / delving** (about 28 times the expected rate)
- **underscore / underscores** (about 14 times)
- **showcase / showcasing** (about 11 times)

Common words with the largest absolute gains:
- **potential, findings, crucial**

The ten-word "common set" used for their estimate:
- across, additionally, comprehensive, crucial, enhancing, exhibited, insights, notably, particularly, within

Other style words named in the study or its sample list:
- intricate, intricacies, meticulous, meticulously, pivotal, realm, commendable, groundbreaking, multifaceted, nuanced, pinpoint, streamline, leverage(s), encompass, bolster, accentuate

## 1.2 Wikipedia "AI vocabulary" list

Main list: additionally (especially at the start of a sentence), align with, boasts (meaning "has"), bolstered, crucial, deep dive, delve, emphasizing, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, highlight (verb), interplay, intricate, landscape (abstract), meticulous, pivotal, robust, showcase, tapestry (abstract), testament, underscore (verb), valuable, vibrant.

The list shifts with each model generation:

| Period | Typical words |
|---|---|
| 2023 to mid-2024 | delve, tapestry, testament, intricate, interplay, meticulously, pivotal, underscore, landscape, garner, vibrant |
| Mid-2024 to mid-2025 | align with, enhance, fostering, highlighting, showcasing, pivotal, underscore, crucial |
| Mid-2025 to mid-2026 | emphasizing, enhance, highlighting, showcasing |
| Mid-2026 onward | challenge, clearer, dependable, echoed, foster, leverage, matters, multifaceted, practical, prioritize, quietly, steady, universally |

Grok overuses: causal, empirical, correlate, underscore.

## 1.3 Significance and legacy phrases

stands as, serves as, is a testament to, plays a crucial / pivotal / vital / key role, underscores its importance, reflects broader trends, setting the stage for, marks a shift, a key turning point, evolving landscape, focal point, indelible mark, deeply rooted, "this distinction matters because", has shaped the conversation about.

## 1.4 Promotional words (older models, 2023 to 2025)

boasts a, vibrant, rich, profound, showcasing, exemplifies, commitment to, nestled, in the heart of, groundbreaking, renowned, featuring, diverse array.

## 1.5 Formal substitutes for plain words

Human writing more often uses the plain verb: *used* not *utilized*, *tried* not *attempted*, *wrote* not *authored*, *has* not *boasts*, *is* not *serves as*.

## 1.6 Common scientific-prose tells (from the studies above)

- Sentence-initial **Additionally, Notably, Furthermore, Moreover, Importantly** used as default connectors
- **comprehensive, robust, novel, significant** used as praise rather than as defined terms ("significant" without a statistical test)
- **valuable insights, sheds light on, paves the way, a deeper understanding**
- **landscape, realm, paradigm** for an ordinary research area
