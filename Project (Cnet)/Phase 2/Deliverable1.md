## Selection and evidence

## Cross-paper synthesis

The studies cover analysis (P1), attacks (P2/P7/P8/P11), monitoring (P3), execution controls (P4–P6), evaluation (P9) and traffic prediction (P10). Their scores measure different tasks and cannot be ranked directly. Our experiment combines flow predictions with explicit action policies, measures useful completion alongside violations, and isolates enforcement by replaying identical proposals. Existing policy defenses rule out claiming the gate itself as novel.
