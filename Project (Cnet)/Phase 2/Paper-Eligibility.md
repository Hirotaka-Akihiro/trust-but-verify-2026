## Conclusion and limits

The list contains three eligible original journal articles and eight additional published research papers under the assignment's explicit conference/journal wording. P4–P6 address enforcement directly; P7–P9/P11 support agent evaluation and attack design, while P10 supports traffic classification. Their transfer to network-policy enforcement is explained rather than treated as an already demonstrated result.

Publication metadata verifies year and venue; it does not substitute for reading an entire paper. Full published text was not accessible for P1–P3 or P5 through the sources used here. Their comparison entries therefore stay within publisher abstracts/section snippets. No unsupported full-text claim or guarantee of the instructor's grading interpretation is made.
