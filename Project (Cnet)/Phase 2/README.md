## Laptop workload

One dataset, up to 10,000 cleaned records, a Decision Tree and 100-tree Random Forest, one 4-bit 3B local model, three policies, three action tools, 20 gate-test cases and 100 cached proposals. Ten pilot queries precede testing. All gate conditions reuse those proposals. There is no LLM training, separate VPN predictor, confidence calibration or live network deployment.

The rebuilt report has 14 pages in Times New Roman and no cover date. Diagrams and research questions match the reduced method. Step headings, model/call counts, fonts and local links were checked.

## Paper checks

The eligibility file records year, publication type, venue, relevance and ranking evidence. Eight additions meet the assignment's published conference/journal route. Original-journal Q1 evidence uses named 2024 SJR snapshots; conference papers are not assigned journal quartiles. Eligibility checks are distinct from full-text access: P1–P3 and published CaMeL summaries remain limited to available publisher evidence.

Original PDFs and the archived earlier literature draft are retained. Experiments remain proposed, not completed.
